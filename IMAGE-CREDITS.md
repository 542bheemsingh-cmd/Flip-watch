# Background Image Credits

Flip Watch uses free-to-use Unsplash photographs as low-opacity background layers. The UI adds dark overlays and crops them responsively for readability.

- [Dark blue grid background](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-dark-blue-background-with-lines-and-dots-11Ayjq5VO-Y) by Jan Habarta, used under the Unsplash License.
- [Intricate pocket watch gears](https://unsplash.com/photos/gold-and-black-leather-textile-u_RiRTA_TtY) by Josh Redd, used under the Unsplash License.
- [Study desk with math notes](https://unsplash.com/s/photos/messy-notes), used under the Unsplash License.
- [Opened book on a dark table](https://unsplash.com/photos/opened-book-LrJOULe72zU), used under the Unsplash License.

The same dark grid and open-book photos are used in the PDF Reader background for its dark and sepia themes.
